# Decisions

One line per methodological decision: what you chose, why, and what it changed.
Agents append here via the `/new-feature` skill; you append here by hand.

- Exclude `casual`, `registered`: they sum to `cnt` exactly. Leaky model R^2 1.000 -> honest ridge 0.583.
- Exclude `cnt_2d_bfr`: a shipped 2-day lag we cannot test the alignment of. Build our own lags instead.
- Exclude `instant`, `yr`, `days_since_2011`: time indexes let a tree predict a flat constant past 2011 and a linear model extrapolate a trend we have no grounds for.
- Split 2011 / H1 2012 / H2 2012 by date. Test set touched once, after all feature work.
- Report mean and persistence baselines with every score. Persistence (test R^2 0.510, MAE 840) is within 0.07 R^2 of ridge and beats it on MAE; that caveat goes in the report.
- 2026-11-25 – **ADDED FEATURE**: `temp_lag_1`, which is yesterday's temperature. It only uses information from the day before, making it available when we make a prediction. It also costs us no extra rows. **REASONING**: Weather tends to carry over from one day to the next. Yesterday's temperature may add context that today's temperature alone does not. **VALIDATION**: Ridge R^2 went from 0.6475 to 0.6951. Its average error went from 832.9 to 746.1 rides. Gradient boosting (hgb) R^2 went from 0.2185 to 0.1936. Its average error went from 1296.6 to 1321.1 rides. **DECISION**: Keep it. **JUSTIFICATION**: Ridge improved by far more than the 0.01 that could just be random noise. Hgb got a little worse, and I am reporting that instead of hiding it. Hgb was already struggling because 2012 ridership is higher than anything it saw in 2011.
- 2026-11-25 – **SELECTED TRANSFORMATION**: Trained gradient boosting (hgb) on the ratio of each day's rides to the 7-day average (`cnt_roll_7`), then multiplied back to get rides. **REASONING**: I was tasked to choose using validation only, so I picked the transformation with the best validation score. If two had scored about the same, I would have taken the simpler one. But the ratio beat the next best (difference from `cnt_roll_7`) by 0.0377 R^2, which is too big to ignore. **VALIDATION**: hgb R^2 went from 0.1936 to 0.7638. Its average error went from 1321.1 to 657.1 rides. The difference version scored 0.7261, the lag-7 difference 0.4955, and the log version 0.1605. **TEST (scored once)**: hgb R^2 went from -0.5785 to 0.6287. Its average error went from 2036.7 to 837.5 rides. Ridge scored 0.6238 (876.6) and persistence scored 0.5099 (840.1). **DECISION**: Defensible, so I keep it behind a flag. The committed `reports/metrics.json` stays the untransformed pipeline. **JUSTIFICATION**: It only uses information from before today. It fixes a known weakness, because trees cannot predict above what they saw in 2011 and rides grew about 65% in 2012. The log version did not help, so the gain comes from removing the level and not from a trick. The caveat is that I chose among four on one validation window, and the test result shows it. The lead over ridge shrank from 0.0687 on validation to 0.0049 on test, and hgb only just beats persistence on average error.
